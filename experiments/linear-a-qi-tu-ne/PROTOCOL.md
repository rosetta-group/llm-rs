# Qi-tu-ne: heading–entry contrast

This is a manual source follow-up to the HT85/117 person-slot audit. Its bounded question
is whether HT7b really places a numeral after qi-tu-ne, and whether that contrast distinguishes
a personal name from an administrative category. It is descriptive development, not a new
statistical or decipherment experiment. Sources are inspected before annotations are frozen.

**Role contrast:** the same sign sequence occupies different written positions.
**Semantic distinction:** evidence selects a referent class, such as person rather than occupation.

## What is supplied

- GORILA I pp.14–15, with photos, drawings, transcriptions and apparatus for both HT7 faces;
  live SigLA word views and drawings; the unchanged pinned corpus.
- Previously archived GORILA I pp.137 and 199 for HT87 and HT117b, inspected again for the
  comparison. These are the other two exact qi-tu-ne word-layer occurrences.
- Eight manual logical-row annotations for HT7a/b, original signs and words, physical lines,
  reading qualifications and three manually reviewed occurrence cases. No person, gender
  or kinship label is supplied. A published VIR on face a is not automatically inherited on b.

## Procedure

```text
Check HT7b's sign sequence and following mark against its photograph and edition
Inspect HT7a for the surrounding account type
Compare qi-tu-ne with the HT87 and HT117b headings
Record reading clarity, overwriting and cross-face assumptions separately
Freeze and commit source snapshots, annotations and descriptive driver
Validate original row/sign coverage and the three literal occurrence identities
Compare rival interpretations without assigning a translation
```

## Decision rules and limits

1. A readable numeral following qi-tu-ne establishes a count-associated occurrence. It
   does not by itself establish that the word names an individual, or what is counted.
2. No aliasing of qi/ki, spelling change or sound-correspondence retest. The compared
   occurrence signs must all be AB21f–AB69–AB24; conventional qi is the label for AB21f.
3. Separate the observable contrast from proposed meanings. A personal name heading a
   supervised group and an occupational/group label heading its members can generate the
   same layout. If both remain compatible, no semantic winner is reported.
4. HT7a/b are one physical object, regardless of the corpus face_count=3 metadata (which
   also has an object-level HT7 container record). The comparison uses HT7, HT87 and HT117.
5. Retain overwritten text as a source qualification. Do not reconstruct erased names,
   create parent-child pairs, assume all sections share units, or infer an unprinted total.
6. Existing source-audit helpers are reused unchanged. No new model, p-value, fitted score
   or recovery gate. The full Davis–Valério chapter was already read in the preceding audit;
   its p.25 conditional interpretation is context, not labelled truth.

The run refuses overwrite. Freeze verification requires the archived local snapshots;
freshly fetched HTML may have different session metadata. Independent expert review remains
absent. The fixed observations were selected from an exposed development concordance.

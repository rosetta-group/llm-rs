## Image requirements

```text
Preserve a full-page image with its caption and page number
Retain tablet edges and all signs around the disputed occurrence
Supply the photograph and drawing separately when both exist
Use a legible scan, preferably 300 dpi or better
Keep source identity and any scale bar with the image
```

An isolated *65 crop is insufficient. Do not clean up, redraw or infer damaged strokes before
recording the original evidence. A scan can show surviving spacing; it cannot supply a missing
right-hand boundary. No absolute millimetre measurement is possible without a reliable scale.

# Theban *65/FAR: the commodity-or-kinship audit

This is a curated source audit of a proposed son formula, not a language-recovery test.
Its purpose is to distinguish evidence about a sign's function from evidence about its meaning.

**Syllabogram:** a sign used for sound; the proposed attached *65 supplies part/all of a son expression.
**Commodity marker:** a sign naming the allocated product; editorial FAR supplies this interpretation.
**Conditional component value:** arithmetic on the supplied readings only, with gaps and doubtful digits unresolved.
**Closed account:** a complete, secure set of allocations with a secure total and known scope.

## Rival readings and source limits

1. **Son reading.** In the selected Theban contexts the disputed glyph belongs to the preceding
   personal expression, and the following amount inherits HORD or VIN. The quantity is food
   allocated to that recipient, not a count of sons. A phonographic function alone would not
   prove the specific son meaning.
2. **Commodity reading.** The glyph introduces FAR as a separate product. A HORD total may omit
   those allocations. This needs a supported accounting convention; missing FAR in a surviving
   total is suggestive, not an arithmetic disproof.
3. **No global substitution.** KN Fs2 and PY Un718 are commodity controls. DĀMOS's FAR spelling
   and whitespace already encode an editorial reading; they cannot independently adjudicate it.
   Gp227's -u-jo comparison has a damaged name and a spelling difference from Fq229. No same-person
   identity is supplied. Gp215 remains a possible directed relation, not a labelled edge.

Select 13 pinned objects: 11 Theban and two outside controls. Fq254[+]255 is one joined object.
Keep complete source transcriptions and meta.Joins. No exhaustive glyph census is claimed.
Source observations and arithmetic discrepancies were explored before this freeze; it is an
exploratory audit, not a prospective blind discovery. Freeze before generating machine outputs.
No source-dependent labels enter any earlier benchmark. No Linear A or Etruscan scoring.

Palaima2003 and James2002-2003 are locally pinned. Duhoux2007 is web-only full text with frozen
manual notes, not a hashed PDF. The original TOP photographs/drawings and AGS2003 full response
were not obtained. Record their arguments only as attributed in the sources actually read;
do not claim independent surface inspection or a complete adjudication of the debate.

## Fixed arithmetic and eligibility

Use dry units BASE=240 Z, T=24 Z, V=4 Z. Never combine liquid and dry quantities or infer litres.
Audit all six total conversions in James p407 Table1, retaining the printed total and converted
number separately. Convert visible/restored components conditionally; a match is not proof
that the tablet's complete total is recovered. For Fq252 keep the supplied cross-line V2 join;
for Fq277 treat leading ]2 as BASE2 only within this component calculation.

For Fq214 and Fq254[+]255 compute the visible disputed-allocation components' shift between
HORD and FAR under the rival readings. Missing quantities contribute no visible component,
not a known zero. Doubtful readings make the shift conditional; breaks make it non-exhaustive.
Do not report these shifts as exact amounts or unconditional lower bounds.
